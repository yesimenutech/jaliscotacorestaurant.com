Our Story

El Auténtico Jalisco in Aurora, IL has been proudly serving authentic Mexican cuisine to the greater Chicago area since 2000. Our menu offers a rich variety of traditional favorites, including tacos, tortas, burritos, sopes, gorditas, quesadillas, tostadas, fajitas, enchiladas, and many more. Whether you are stopping in for a quick bite or enjoying a full family meal, every dish is prepared with bold, authentic flavors and the freshest ingredients.

We proudly serve guests from Naperville, Oswego, Montgomery, North Aurora, Batavia, Sugar Grove, Yorkville, and surrounding communities. Experience the true taste of Mexico at El Auténtico Jalisco and enjoy a meal that will keep you coming back for more.
